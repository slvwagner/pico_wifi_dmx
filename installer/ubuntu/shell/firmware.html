Install firmware on a Pico 2 W

The application and separate Wi-Fi firmware are bundled and validated before use. Flash one Pico at a time.
Installed firmware

Check Picos found by the Controller and compare them with the bundled version.

Put the target Pico into BOOTSEL mode 1. Disconnect every other Pico from this computer. 2. Unplug the target Pico's USB cable. 3. Press and hold BOOTSEL. 4. Reconnect USB while holding BOOTSEL. 5. Release BOOTSEL when Ubuntu detects the Pico. Do not disconnect power or USB after flashing starts.
